About & sources

AIRFRAME is a lightweight, dependency-free aircraft explorer. Everything runs in your browser; favorites and comparisons are kept in local storage.

Data

Figures are rounded, family-level approximations for orientation only. Military performance is left blank because published figures vary widely. Verify against manufacturer or regulator documentation before relying on any number.

Artwork

Aircraft drawings are stylized class illustrations, not accurate depictions of each model.

Safety
